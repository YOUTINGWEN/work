小誌/文本
Sculpture

紙張、手縫書

2025

將錄像中的聲音與影像變為在紙上的圖片

Within the space, through casting and assembling, matter is continuously commodified into the character “售” (sold).
At the same time, I repeatedly shatter these concrete forms. When the character “售” loses its shape and becomes unrecognizable, all that remains on the ground is a pile of gray dust and fragments. On site, processes of reproduction, assembly, recognition, destruction, and illegibility unfold in constant shifts—an ongoing experiment in transformation.
