Sculpture

現成物（使用過的鷹架）、混凝土

2025

我們要如何討論改變？
是不是應該足夠坦然才算是勇敢的人。
如果變化讓我感覺失去，可不可以不要改變。
我透過採樣的行為消化這些即將到來的地景變化，
也正在書寫自己的環境評估報告。
今年從插秧到收成，我採了混入水泥的泥土；採了阿嬤的看法；用跑步採了出售中的風景。

How should we talk about change?Perhaps only by being candid enough can one be called brave.
If change makes me feel a sense of loss, is it possible not to change?
Through the act of sampling, I digest these impending transformations of the landscape,
while also writing my own environmental report.
This year, from transplanting rice seedlings to harvest, I sampled soil mixed with cement;
I sampled my grandmother’s perspective;
I sampled the scenery of land being sold, through running.
