游婷雯 You Ting Wen

1997年 出生於台灣, 宜蘭

有感於家鄉的地景變化，農地販售後改建為農舍；插上廣告立牌的土地；建地被建商購買後的大型建案。於是我以藝術創作為路徑，開啟土地與自身經驗的討論。作品持續面對人與土地的複雜性，並不倦地向非人物質提問。

Grew up in Sanxing Township, Yilan, where an acute awareness of shifting landscapes led me to use art as a way to explore the relationship between land and personal experience. My practice continually engages with the complexities of land, questioning and responding to it through creation.
